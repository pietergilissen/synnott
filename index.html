The White Mountains

Home to Some of the Northeast's Best Climbing

We guide across the region's classic terrain, from the granite of Cathedral and Whitehorse Ledges to the alpine ice of Cannon Cliff and Mount Washington's ravines.

  • Cathedral Ledge & Whitehorse Ledge — classic New Hampshire rock
  • Cannon Cliff — big multi-pitch rock and ice, including Black Dike
  • Frankenstein Cliff & Mount Willard — popular ice climbing venues
  • Mount Washington — Tuckerman & Huntington Ravines, winter ascents
  • NH 4,000-Footers & Presidential Traverse — guided hiking objectives
