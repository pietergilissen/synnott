About Synnott Mountain Guides

Home / About

Guiding the White Mountains Since 1996

Synnott Mountain Guides (SMG) is a mountain guide service operating out of New Hampshire's White Mountains, offering private and group instruction and guiding in climbing, mountaineering, backcountry skiing, hiking, and avalanche education for all ability levels.

SMG is owned and operated by Mark Synnott, an AMGA/IFMGA-certified mountain guide and member of The North Face Climbing Team, who has been leading climbing trips since 1996. An accomplished international climber, Mark has led and participated in expeditions from Yosemite to Pakistan, Baffin Island to the Pitcairn Islands, bringing decades of real-world alpine experience to every course SMG teaches.

SMG's guiding roster includes experienced, AMGA-trained instructors who bring the same commitment to safety, technical skill, and genuine enthusiasm for the mountains to every client — from first-time climbers to those training for expeditions like Mount Rainier.

Mark Synnott, IFMGA Mountain Guide
