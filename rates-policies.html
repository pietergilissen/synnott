Policies

Weather & Cancellations: Winter courses rarely cancel for snow — winter conditions are part of the experience. If a day is a complete loss, we cancel or reschedule, and any deposit paid stands as credit toward a future course. In summer, steady rain can occasionally shut down climbing; we cover ground school and technical topics indoors and reassess conditions through the day.
Flexible Booking Deposit: A $100 deposit secures a spot in our group-matching system. If we can't fill your group, you may choose a refund, a credit toward a future course, or convert to a private booking at the private rate.
