The Ultimate Northeast Objective

Mount Washington is the highest peak in New Hampshire and the entire Northeast. It's a serious and rewarding mountaineering objective in its own right, and the perfect stepping stone for climbers preparing for bigger peaks like Mount Rainier.

Our guided winter climbs are led by AMGA/IFMGA-certified guides who know the mountain's notorious weather and terrain intimately, including Tuckerman Ravine, Huntington Ravine, and the Lion Head route.

This course is available as a private trip, a pre-existing group, or via our Flexible Booking option, where we try to match you with other climbers.
