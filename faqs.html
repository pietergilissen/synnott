Frequently Asked Questions

The more notice you give us, the greater the likelihood we can accommodate exactly what you're looking for — guides can get booked up by other parties. Don't wait for a perfect forecast; book your dates, then call a couple of days out to discuss weather options. That said, never rule out a last-minute request — we sometimes have openings or cancellations.

We usually meet at our office in Intervale, NH at Ragged Mountain Equipment, 279 NH-16, Intervale, NH 03845. Your guide may suggest a different meeting spot depending on where you'll be climbing. For climbs at Cannon Cliff, we meet at the parking area below the north end of the cliff. If you don't know the way, we'll send you a map and directions.

For most courses, the normal meeting time is 8:00am, though your guide might set a different time. We normally climb a full day, finishing between 4 and 5pm — we're often the first group at the cliff and the last to leave. If you need to be on the road at a particular time, just let your guide know in the morning.

All technical equipment is included in the cost of every course — we have a full fleet of boots, rock shoes, crampons, harnesses, axes, and helmets. If you're interested in buying your own gear, we recommend taking your course first so you know exactly what you need. Feel free to bring your own gear if it's appropriate for the course.

For rock climbing, dress as if headed on a day hike — t-shirt, shorts, socks, and sneakers (no sandals) if warm, plus a pullover or windbreaker if cooler, and a rain shell just in case. For winter courses, dress as if going skiing: your own long underwear and socks, light insulating layers, fleece jacket, shell pants, and a windshell with a hood. You'll also need a puffy jacket (we have loaners), gloves and spares, hat, and goggles. Let us know ahead of time if you're missing anything — don't wait until the morning of your course.

For winter courses, this is rarely a problem since snowstorms are part of the mountaineering experience — we simply go about our business. If a day looks like a complete loss, we cancel or reschedule, and any deposit stands as credit toward a future course. In summer, steady rain can occasionally shut things down, especially for intermediate climbers, but intro courses cover ground school regardless, and more advanced courses can shift to technical topics like anchor-building and self-rescue in a dry spot while we wait for conditions to improve.

Contact SMG for a list of recommended places to stay, including local B&Bs, hostels, motels, and camping in the White Mountain National Forest or at local campgrounds. North Conway is a busy tourist destination with all the usual chains represented — Holiday Inn Express, Comfort Inn, Hamptons, and more.

This depends on the course, but generally you need to be fit enough to hike around for most of a day with a moderate pack. We can usually set an agenda that fits your fitness level. The two courses where fitness is a bigger factor are Mount Washington ascents and Presidential Traverses — both require being a strong hiker capable of grinding uphill for most of a day with a pack. If you're concerned about your fitness, let us know and we can talk through a training plan together.

Still have questions? Contact us — we're happy to help you plan.
